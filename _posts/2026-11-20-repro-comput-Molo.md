*"It runs on my machine" - experiences from the SwissRN workshops on Computational Reproducibility*

**Fabio Molo** du réseau suisse de reproductibilité [SwissRN](https://www.swissrn.org/). 
Fabio Molo est manager du [CRS](https://www.swissrn.org/) (Center for Reproducible Science and Research Synthesis) de l'Université de Zurich. 

*Abstract:*

*Computational reproducibility is still treated as a nice-to-have in many research projects, even in computationally simple empirical data analyses. With a series of hands-on workshops across Swiss universities, the Swiss RN walks participants through a full, modern, end‑to‑end reproducible pipeline — not just theory, and not just tools in isolation. We cover version control with Git, reproducible reports with Quarto (R or Python), analysis workflow managers, reproducible computational environments with Docker, and best practices for putting it all together and publishing reproducible data analyses. As one of the instructors in these workshops, I will provide an overview of this SwissRN project: how we obtained funding, the teaching environments we use, experiences from the exchange with empirical researchers, and the open educational resources we created [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22281069](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22281069).*
